In the act of exchange, the use-value of that thing becomes a counterfactual—*could’ve been*—thanks to the presence of money. #develop 

“In the money economy, the problem of exchange-value is simplified, since it will be exchange for *money* that will be especially important. The utility on his value scale of the highest direct use-value will be compared to the utility of the sum of money the good could procure in exchange.”
