Producers’ goods include labor, land, and capital goods.

“For a man to produce a consumers’ good, he must obtain labor services and the services of land and capital goods, in order to use the technological “know-how” available in the production of the good. **The capitalists are the only ones who *spend money on producers’ goods***.”
