Inelastic goods are those that people will buy regardless of the price. A unitary value scale means all goods are compared—goods become more substitutable with money, to some degree. The more diverse an economy, the less likely it is that any good remains truly inelastic.

“There is a tendency for the demands for the various consumers’ goods to become more elastic.”
