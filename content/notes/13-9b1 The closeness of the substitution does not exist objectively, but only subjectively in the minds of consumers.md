As with values, the closeness of goods does not exist objectively, but subjectively in the minds of consumers (which gets aggregated inter-subjectively, as it were).

“**The closeness of the substitution depends, however, on the particular circumstances of the consumer and his preferences rather than on technological similarity**.”
