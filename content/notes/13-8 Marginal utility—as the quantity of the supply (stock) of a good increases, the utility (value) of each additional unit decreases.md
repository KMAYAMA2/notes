Scarcity *per se* does not equal value, since anything can be substituted by any other things (e.g., see [[Eric Voskuil |Voskuil]]’s *Scarcity Fallacy* and *Substitution Principle*, respectively).

“**The least important end fulfilled by the stock is** known as the *satisfaction provided by the marginal unit*, or the *utility of the marginal unit*—in short: the marginal satisfaction, or ***marginal utility***.”

“**The law of marginal utility depends on no physiological or psychological assumptions but is based on the praxeological truth that the first unit of a good will be used to satisfy the most urgent want, the second unit the next most urgent want, etc.**”
