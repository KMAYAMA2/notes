> “Yes indeed you see, we all see, but often you do not observe.” – Joseph Bell

> “You see, but you do not observe. The distinction is clear. For example, you have frequently seen the steps which lead up from the hall to this room.” – [[Sherlock Holmes]]

> “More is missed by not looking than not knowing.” – Thomas McCrae

> “For one mistake made for not knowing, ten mistakes are made for not looking.” – James Alexander Lindsay

> “To observe correctly and decide wrongly is sure to happen to the best of us, but to observe carelessly happens only when we permit it.” – Thomas McCrae

> “To observe attentively is to remember distinctly” – Edgar Allan Poe

> “I think that I am superior to the common run of men in noticing things which easily escape attention, and in observing them carefully.” – Charles Darwin

> “You can observe a lot by just watching.” – Yogi Berra

As Popper famously argued, you don’t just “observe” the world as it is, because your observation necessarily reflects your worldview. The essence of observation is self-reflection. #revisit
