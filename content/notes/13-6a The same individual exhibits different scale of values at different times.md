To be more precise, the real act implies a value scale at the moment—but this does not guarantee that the actor will behave similarly in the future, given similar circumstances, unless [[13-6a2 Knowledge keeps you the same, from the perspective of exhibited value scales |mediated by knowledge]]. **Humans are unpredictable because we create knowledge, and knowledge can be only ascertained through actions—we are doubly blind, as it were**.
