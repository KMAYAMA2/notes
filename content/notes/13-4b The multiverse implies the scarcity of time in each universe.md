The *general condition* (the part of environment that cannot be controlled by the actor, to use the term as defined by [[Murray Rothbard |Rothbard]] himself) of the multiverse implies the scarcity of time for each universe. **The immortal being has unlimited time in his universe, but his time is still scarce because he cannot realize all the cases of *it could’ve been otherwise***—which are realized in the multiverse—in his timeline, because even if he achieves everything, the order at which his ends were satisfied must be different when compared to that of other universes.
