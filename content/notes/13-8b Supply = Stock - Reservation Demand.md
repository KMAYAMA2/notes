Because (a) Stock - Supply = Reservation Demand means (b) **Supply = Stock - Reservation Demand**. 

**If there is no reservation demand, then the supply curve will be vertical**.

“The supply curve is reducible to a *reservation demand curve* and to a *quantity of physical stock*.”

“If reservation demand is zero, all stock is sold at any price (vertical supply curve). Thus, **supply is essentially the inverse of reservation demand**.”
