“It is the *time element*, the result of the various individuals’ time preferences, and *not* the alleged independent productivity of capital goods, from which the interest rate and interest income arise.”

“Capital goods are vital and of crucial importance in production, but their production is, in the long run, imputable to land, labor, and time factors—**the capitalists’ function is thus a *time* function**.”
