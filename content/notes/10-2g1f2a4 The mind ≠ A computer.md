I think we should sub-categorize the mind into human intelligence and artificial intelligence. Only then would both <u>Human intelligence ≠ A computer</u> ([[Daniel Everett]]) and <u>Artificial Intelligence = A computer</u> (Elon Musk) hold. Capability wise, both intelligence might be [[How Popper would use LLMs |the same]]. But human intelligence evolves while LLMs start with fixed base. Maybe there lies the functional difference between the two. #develop
